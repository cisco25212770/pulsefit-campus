How the 6-Step System Works

  • 1. Register or Log In: Authenticates your personal student profile so targets and logs stay linked to your account.
  • 2. Set Fitness Goals: Calculates or customizes daily calorie, protein, carb, fat, and weekly workout targets.
  • 3. Record Workout: Logs exercises, sets, reps, and weights with automatic volume load tracking.
  • 4. Record Meals: Captures food items, portion sizes, and meal times throughout the day.
  • 5. Review Estimates: Automatically computes calorie and protein estimates per meal for student review.
  • 6. Check Dashboard: Visualizes daily macro adherence and multi-week workout & weight trends.
